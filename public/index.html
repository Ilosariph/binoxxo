How to play
  • Fill every cell with X or O.
  • Never more than two equal symbols next to each other — in a row or a column.
  • Every row and every column holds as many X as O.
  • No two rows are identical, and no two columns are.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved without guessing.
